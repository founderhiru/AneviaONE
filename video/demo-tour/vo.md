# AneviaONE demo tour — voice-over script (calm, composed, Indian English; ~1:50)
01 (0:00–0:12) Your health has a history. But today it lives in pieces: reports in a drawer, results in an inbox, prescriptions in a photo roll. AneviaONE brings it together.
02 (0:12–0:26) Start with a report you already have. Upload a PDF, take a photo, or share it straight from WhatsApp or Gmail. Your original is always kept.
03 (0:26–0:42) With your permission, AneviaONE reads it, and turns dates, results and units into clear, organised information. Every result links back to the page it came from. Anything uncertain is held for you to confirm.
04 (0:42–0:54) Each report joins one timeline. The same test is connected across documents and years, so your history grows with you.
05 (0:54–1:04) See what changed since your last report, and how each measure is moving over time.
06 (1:04–1:16) Before an appointment, open For Doctor. A clear one-page summary of your history, ready to share.
07 (1:16–1:32) And you're not alone in this. Keep records for a parent or a child you care for. Or invite an adult relative to share theirs. They approve every request, and can withdraw access at any time.
08 (1:32–1:42) Your health information belongs to you. Delete a report, or your entire account, whenever you choose.
09 (1:42–1:50) AneviaONE. Every record. Every change. One intelligent health history.
